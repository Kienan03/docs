Every interaction with a Hooper should look like a professional basketball training relationship, whatever the Hooper's age. Here's what that rules out.

## Not allowed

- Sexual, romantic, suggestive, harassing, threatening, discriminatory, abusive, or otherwise inappropriate conduct with a Hooper.
- Contacting a minor outside Hooper Howse or meeting a minor in person, except as the [youth protection rules](/trainers/working-with-minors) allow.
- Pursuing unrelated personal relationships with Hoopers, or using Hooper Howse messages to sell unrelated things.
- False or misleading claims about your qualifications, experience, certifications, basketball history, or services.
- Posting, sending, or asking for sexual, violent, hateful, or otherwise rule-breaking content.
- Fake accounts, fake reviews, or manipulating ratings, rankings, or subscription counts.
- Sharing your account or letting anyone else coach under your profile.

## Keep paid training on Hooper Howse

You're free to run your own training business and work with other platforms. What you can't do is use Hooper Howse, its Hooper information, or a relationship that started on Hooper Howse to move a Hooper's similar paid training off the platform or around its payment system. This applies while you're a Trainer and for **12 months** after you leave.

If you trained someone before they subscribed to you through Hooper Howse, you can keep training them outside it, as long as you don't use Hooper Howse information or the platform relationship to work around it.

## Your content and credentials

Only submit content you have the rights to. Don't use a Hooper's videos or likeness in your own marketing without the written consent described in [Hooper privacy](/trainers/hooper-privacy). Keep your credentials and profile accurate, and fix anything that becomes wrong.

## Enforcement

Hooper Howse can investigate reported violations and suspend or remove Trainers. Any suspected inappropriate conduct involving a minor results in immediate suspension during the investigation, and removal if a violation is confirmed.
