Every interaction with a Hooper should look like a professional basketball training relationship, whatever the Hooper's age. These rules apply to every Hooper you work with on Hooper Howse. Hoopers under 18 have additional rules in [Working with minors](/trainers/working-with-minors).

## Keep it professional

Every message must relate to basketball training, coaching, development, scheduling, or another legitimate Hooper Howse purpose. Never:

- Send anything romantic, sexual, suggestive, flirtatious, harassing, threatening, discriminatory, or abusive, or act that way toward a Hooper.
- Comment on a Hooper's body or appearance unless it's about basketball technique.
- Pursue unrelated personal relationships with Hoopers, or use Hooper Howse messages to sell unrelated things.
- Offer gifts, money, or special favors outside the Hooper Howse service.
- Post, send, or ask for sexual, violent, hateful, or otherwise rule-breaking content.

## Keep communication on Hooper Howse

These rules apply to any Hooper you connect with through Hooper Howse. They don't apply to relationships you already had before that Hooper found you on Hooper Howse.

- Don't ask a Hooper for their phone number, personal email, social media handle, or other contact info through Hooper Howse.
- Don't share yours to move the conversation or training off the platform.

## Personal information and media

- Only ask for what you need to coach. Don't ask for a home address, school schedule, or location.
- Never request, send, or keep sexual, intimate, suggestive, or otherwise inappropriate photos or videos of a Hooper. Keep training videos to what coaching requires.

## Honest accounts and credentials

- No false or misleading claims about your qualifications, experience, certifications, basketball history, or services.
- No fake accounts, fake reviews, or manipulating ratings, rankings, or subscription counts.
- Don't share your account or let anyone else coach under your profile.

## Keep paid training on Hooper Howse

You can't use Hooper Howse, its Hooper information, or a relationship that started on Hooper Howse to move a Hooper's similar paid training off the platform or around its payment system. This applies while you're a Trainer and for **12 months** after you leave.

## Can I train clients outside Hooper Howse?

Yes. You can keep running your own training business and work with other platforms. If you trained someone before they subscribed to you through Hooper Howse, you can keep training them outside it.

The limit is the rule above: you can't use Hooper Howse, its Hooper information, or a relationship that started here to pull a Hooper's paid training off the platform.

## Your content and credentials

Only submit content you have the rights to. Don't use a Hooper's videos or likeness in your own marketing without the written consent described in [Hooper privacy](/trainers/hooper-privacy). Keep your credentials and profile accurate, and fix anything that becomes wrong.

## Enforcement

Hooper Howse can investigate reported violations and suspend or remove Trainers. Any suspected inappropriate conduct involving a minor results in immediate suspension during the investigation, and removal if a violation is confirmed.
