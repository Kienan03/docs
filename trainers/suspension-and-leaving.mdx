Your Trainer Agreement runs until you or Hooper Howse ends it. Short of that, Hooper Howse can pause new signups to you or suspend you while it looks into a problem.

## Pauses

Hooper Howse can stop new Hoopers from subscribing to you at any time, for example because of capacity, quality, or a pending review. Your current Hoopers keep their subscriptions during a pause.

## Suspension and removal

You can be suspended or removed for breaking the Trainer Agreement or the other Trainer documents, misconduct, safety concerns, not delivering GameChecks or Drills, payment or fraud concerns, or other reasons the agreements allow. Serious safety issues and suspected inappropriate conduct involving a minor can mean immediate suspension.

If you're removed, Hooper Howse can turn off your profile, messages, and platform access right away. Your Hoopers stop being billed for you and are asked to pick another Trainer. You keep eligible earnings from before the removal, but nothing from subscriptions that ended because of it.

## Leaving on your own

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give 30 days' notice">
    Tell Hooper Howse through the app or by emailing [support@hooperhowse.com](mailto:support@hooperhowse.com). You don't need a reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep coaching through the notice period">
    Keep delivering GameChecks and Drills to your current Hoopers while Hooper Howse moves them to other Trainers or ends their subscriptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete Hooper information">
    Delete any Hooper information stored outside Hooper Howse, as described in [Hooper privacy](/trainers/hooper-privacy).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Hooper Howse can also end the agreement without a reason on 30 days' notice.

## After you leave

- Your final share is counted through your last day and paid on the next monthly payout after Hooper Howse receives the money.
- The rule against moving Hooper Howse Hoopers' paid training off the platform lasts 12 months after you leave. See [Code of conduct](/trainers/code-of-conduct).

## When the rules change

Hooper Howse gives at least 30 days' notice before changing the Trainer Agreement or Trainer documents. If you don't agree, you can leave before the change takes effect, and the old terms apply during your notice period. Changes required by law or needed to protect Hooper safety can take effect right away.
