For every Hooper subscribed to you, you deliver 2 things: GameChecks when they send Skill videos, and at least 1 Drill sent each month. You decide what goes into both.

## What is a GameCheck?

A GameCheck is a short selfie video, about 30 seconds long, that you record in response to a Hooper's Skill videos.

In it, you give personal feedback on the Skills they showed you: what they're doing well, what to fix, and how to fix it. The goal is to help that Hooper get better.

Each week, a Hooper sends you 3 Skill videos, about 1 minute total: Shooting (30 seconds), Finishing (15 seconds), and Dribbling (15 seconds). Once you receive that week's videos, you have **24 hours** to send back their GameCheck.

## What is a Drill?

A Drill is a focused video, about 1 to 2 minutes long, that you create for your Drill library. Each Drill covers 1 specific skill or technique. In it, you:

- Demonstrate and perform the Drill yourself.
- Explain how to do it.
- Explain what it's for and why it matters.

Record every Drill with your phone held vertically (portrait orientation).

Send at least **1 Drill per month** to each Hooper.

### Why you send Drills

Drills give Hoopers something to work on every day between GameChecks. Practicing your Drills daily is how they improve their Skills.

## Your Drill library

Before you go live, you upload a library of at least **30 Drill videos**, each about 1 to 2 minutes long and recorded in vertical phone orientation. There's no maximum.

Every Drill you add later, including ones made for a specific Hooper, also needs to be recorded vertically.

After you're live, you can keep making new Drills to build out your library. You can also create a Drill for a specific Hooper's needs: add it to your library, then share it with that Hooper. See [Become a Trainer](/trainers/become-a-trainer).

## Time commitment

At the 200-Hooper soft cap, expect roughly **15 or more hours a week**. That's an estimate, not a guarantee. You choose when you work, as long as you meet the 24-hour GameCheck window.

## If you're running late

An occasional delay from something outside your control isn't a problem, as long as you send the feedback as soon as you can. Missing the 24-hour window repeatedly or for a long stretch, or not sending a GameCheck or Drill at all, counts as a material failure.

For a material failure, Hooper Howse can withhold your share for that Hooper for that period and can suspend or remove you.

## Safety and video format

When you design Drills and give feedback, follow the [Trainer Safety Standards](/trainers/training-safety).

Record GameChecks and Drills in vertical phone orientation.
