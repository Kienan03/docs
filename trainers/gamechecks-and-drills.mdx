For every Hooper subscribed to you, you deliver two things: GameChecks when they send skill videos, and at least one drill sent each month. You decide what goes into both.

## What is a GameCheck?

A GameCheck is a short video, about 30 seconds long, that you record in response to a Hooper's skill videos.

In it, you give personal feedback on the skills they showed you: what they're doing well, what to fix, and how to fix it. The goal is to help that Hooper get better.

Each week, a Hooper can send you up to three skill videos. Once they finish that week's videos, you have **24 hours** to send back their GameCheck.

If a Hooper doesn't send any videos that week, you don't owe a GameCheck.

## Drills

Send at least **one drill per month** to each Hooper, about 1 to 2 minutes long. This applies every month, whether or not the Hooper sent videos.

## Your drill library

Before you go live, you upload a library of at least **30 drill videos**, each about 1 to 2 minutes long. There's no maximum. See [Become a Trainer](/trainers/become-a-trainer).

## Time commitment

At the 200-Hooper soft cap, expect roughly **15 or more hours a week**. That's an estimate, not a guarantee. You choose when you work, as long as you meet the 24-hour GameCheck window.

## If you're running late

An occasional delay from something outside your control isn't a problem, as long as you send the feedback as soon as you can. Missing the 24-hour window repeatedly or for a long stretch, or not sending a GameCheck or drill at all, counts as a material failure.

For a material failure, Hooper Howse can withhold your share for that Hooper for that period and can suspend or remove you.

## Safety and video format

When you design drills and give feedback, follow the [Trainer Safety Standards](/trainers/training-safety).

Record GameChecks and drills with your phone held upright (portrait mode).
