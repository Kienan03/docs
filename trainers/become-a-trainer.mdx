To coach on Hooper Howse, you need to meet a few requirements, pass a background check, and accept the Trainer documents. Once your profile is approved, you set up payouts and upload your drill library. Uploading your drills is the last step before you go live and start taking Hoopers.

## Requirements

- **Be at least 18 years old.**
- **Have high school, collegiate, or pro experience.** You must have played, coached, or trained basketball at the high school, collegiate, or professional level.
- **Give accurate information.** Your credentials, certifications, basketball history, and qualifications must be accurate and not misleading.
- **Pass the background check.** Hooper Howse screens every Trainer through Checkr before approval. You pay for the check, about \$35, and for the yearly recheck after that. See [Background checks](/trainers/background-checks).
- **Accept the Trainer documents.** You accept the Trainer Agreement and the separate safety, conduct, youth protection, and privacy documents during onboarding.

## After you're approved

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your free months start">
    Your first three months on Hooper Howse have no platform fee. They start on the date your profile is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your pricing tier">
    Your pricing tier (High School, Collegiate, or Pro) is set by the highest level you've played, coached, or trained at. You don't set subscription prices yourself. See [Pricing and payouts](/trainers/pricing-and-payouts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up payouts">
    Complete payout onboarding with Stripe Connect and enter your tax information there. Payouts can be held until this is done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your drill library and go live">
    Upload at least **30 drill videos**, each about 1 to 2 minutes long and filmed vertically. There's no maximum. This is the last step: once your drills are uploaded, you're a live Trainer and Hoopers can subscribe to you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep your profile current

Keep any license or certification you list up to date. Tell Hooper Howse right away about any change that makes your profile inaccurate, including any criminal charge or conviction after you're approved. Email [support@hooperhowse.com](mailto:support@hooperhowse.com).
