Hoopers pay a monthly subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play. You earn a fixed monthly amount for each Hooper subscribed to you, based on your tier. After your first 3 months, a \$20 monthly platform fee comes out of your payout. Payouts go out once a month through Stripe Connect.

## Tiers and what you earn

Your tier is set by the highest level you've played, coached, or trained basketball at: high school, collegiate, or professional. For example, if you played in college and now coach high school, you're in the Collegiate tier. You don't set prices.

| Tier | You earn per Hooper, per month | Example at 200 Hoopers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High School | \$14 | \$2,800 a month |
| Collegiate | \$19 | \$3,800 a month |
| Pro | \$25 | \$5,000 a month |

The 200-Hooper examples are before the \$20 platform fee.

Hooper Howse can change tier amounts or your tier with at least 30 days' notice. Price changes for existing subscribers also follow Apple and Google rules.

## How many Hoopers you can train

There's a soft cap of **200 Hoopers** per Trainer. It's a guideline, not a hard limit.

At 200 Hoopers, expect to spend roughly **15 or more hours a week** on GameChecks and Drills. That's an estimate, not a guarantee.

## Platform fee

After your first 3 months, you pay a **\$20 per month** platform fee. The 3 free months start the day your profile is approved.

The fee comes out of your monthly payout. If a payout is smaller than the fee, the rest carries over to your next payouts. Hooper Howse can change the fee with at least 30 days' notice.

## Getting paid

Payouts run monthly through Stripe Connect. To get paid, finish Stripe's onboarding and enter your tax and payout details directly with Stripe. Each payout shows what you were paid and any deductions.

<Info>
  You only get paid for a Hooper when Hooper Howse gets paid for that Hooper. If Apple or Google pays Hooper Howse late, your payout for those Hoopers goes out on the next monthly payout after the money arrives.
</Info>

## When payouts can change or be held

- **Setup isn't finished.** Payouts can be held until Stripe onboarding and your tax information are complete.
- **Refunds and chargebacks.** If a Hooper's payment is refunded, reversed, or charged back after you've been paid, that amount can come out of a future payout.
- **Missed services.** If you materially fail to deliver GameChecks or Drills for a Hooper, your share for that Hooper for that period can be withheld.
- **Suspension.** While you're suspended during an investigation, payouts can be held. If no violation is found, the held amount is released on the next payout.

## Taxes

You're responsible for your own federal, state, and local taxes. Hooper Howse doesn't withhold employment taxes. When the law requires it, such as backup withholding, amounts can be withheld from payouts, and tax forms are issued as required.
