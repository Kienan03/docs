Hooper information is anything about a Hooper you get through Hooper Howse: names, profile details, messages, Skill videos, GameChecks, and more. Use it only to coach that Hooper through Hooper Howse.

## What you can't do with it

- Build marketing lists, or sell or rent the information.
- Contact Hoopers outside Hooper Howse for unrelated reasons, or use it for unrelated business.
- Share it with another person or business unless an authorized Hooper Howse purpose requires it and the law allows it.
- Upload it, including videos, to outside apps or AI tools that aren't part of Hooper Howse.

## Videos stay on Hooper Howse

Don't download, screen-record, screenshot, save, or repost a Hooper's videos or images outside Hooper Howse. The only exception is when you need to in order to prepare a GameCheck, and then you delete it promptly.

To use a Hooper's videos, images, or name in your own marketing or social media, you need the Hooper's written consent, plus a parent's or guardian's written consent if the Hooper is a minor.

Take extra care with information about Hoopers ages 13 to 17, and don't ask minors for personal information you don't need.

## Keep your account secure

- Use a screen lock on every device you use for Hooper Howse.
- Use a Hooper Howse password you don't use anywhere else.
- Never share your login.
- Don't try to get around security controls or access information beyond what your role needs.

## If something goes wrong

Tell Hooper Howse **within 24 hours** of finding any suspected unauthorized access, loss, theft, or misuse of Hooper information. That includes losing a phone or other device you use for Hooper Howse. Email [support@hooperhowse.com](mailto:support@hooperhowse.com).

## When a Hooper leaves or you leave

When a Hooper's subscription with you ends, or your Trainer Agreement ends, delete any Hooper information you have stored outside Hooper Howse. Don't keep copies unless the law requires it or Hooper Howse asks you to preserve it for an investigation.
