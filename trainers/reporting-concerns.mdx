Report concerns to Hooper Howse through the in-app reporting tool or by emailing [support@hooperhowse.com](mailto:support@hooperhowse.com).

<Danger>
  If a Hooper is in immediate danger or describes a medical emergency, call 911 or local emergency services first. Then tell Hooper Howse.
</Danger>

## What to report and when

| Concern | When to report |
| --- | --- |
| Suspected inappropriate conduct involving a minor, by anyone | Immediately |
| Content that appears to be sexual content involving a minor | Immediately. Don't download, save, forward, or share it. |
| Other safety concerns | Promptly |
| Lost or stolen device, or suspected misuse of Hooper information | Within 24 hours |

## Keep the evidence

Don't delete messages or content connected to something you've reported.

## Reports required by law

Reporting to Hooper Howse doesn't replace any report the law requires you to make yourself, such as to law enforcement or child protective services. If you're legally required to report, you still have to.
