Hooper Howse is the basketball community and training platform. As a Trainer, you coach Hoopers remotely: they subscribe to you, send in Skill videos, and you send back personalized video feedback (GameChecks) and Drills you create.

## Your weekly training cycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hoopers send their Skill videos">
    Every week, each Hooper subscribed to you sends 3 Skill videos, about 1 minute total:

    - **Shooting:** 30 seconds
    - **Finishing:** 15 seconds
    - **Dribbling:** 15 seconds
  </Step>
  <Step title="You send back a GameCheck">
    Within **24 hours** of receiving that week's videos, you record a GameCheck: a short selfie video, about 30 seconds long, with personal feedback on their Skills. Tell them what they're doing well, what to fix, and how to fix it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You send Drills">
    At least once a month, you send each Hooper a Drill from your library. You can add new Drills to your library anytime, including ones made for a specific Hooper's needs. Each Drill is a 1 to 2 minute video where you demonstrate 1 skill or technique, explain how to do it, and explain what it's for.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [GameChecks and Drills](/trainers/gamechecks-and-drills) for the full details.

## Who you train

You can train Hoopers ages 13 and up. Hooper Howse is 13+ only, so no one under 13 can use the app or subscribe to you.

Your Hoopers can include teens ages 13 to 17, so the rules for working with minors apply to you from day one. See [Working with minors](/trainers/working-with-minors).

## How the relationship works

You are an independent contractor, not a Hooper Howse employee. That means:

- You decide the coaching itself: which Skills, Drills, techniques, and feedback each Hooper gets.
- You choose when and where you work, as long as you meet the response times in [GameChecks and Drills](/trainers/gamechecks-and-drills).
- You don't get wages or employee benefits, and you handle your own taxes.

Hooper Howse runs the platform, makes your services available to Hoopers, collects subscription payments through the Apple App Store and Google Play, and pays you your share each month. See [Pricing and payouts](/trainers/pricing-and-payouts).

## Documents you accept

Before you're approved, you review and accept these documents during onboarding:

- Trainer Agreement
- Background Check Disclosure and Authorization
- Minor & Youth Protection Requirements
- Trainer Safety Standards
- Trainer Standards / Code of Conduct
- Privacy / Data Requirements for Trainers

<Note>
  The pages in this help center explain those documents in plain English. If anything here differs from the documents you accepted, the documents control.
</Note>
