Every Trainer must pass a background check before approval. Hooper Howse uses Checkr, Inc., a consumer reporting agency, to run it. The check only runs after you've received the Background Check Disclosure and given your authorization.

## Cost

You pay for your own background check. It costs about **\$35**.

## What a report can include

As allowed by law, a report can include:

- Criminal history
- Sex offender registry status
- Identity verification
- Other background information the law permits

## When checks happen

Hooper Howse runs a check before approving you. If you're approved, it can also run checks during your time as a Trainer, as the law allows, and may ask for an updated screening when reasonably appropriate. Your authorization covers both.

## If a report affects your eligibility

Any decision not to approve you, or to remove you, based on a report follows the law, including any notice you're owed before and after the decision.
