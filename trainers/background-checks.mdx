Every Trainer must pass a background check before approval and again every year after that. Hooper Howse uses Checkr, Inc., a consumer reporting agency, to run it. The check only runs after you've received the Background Check Disclosure and given your authorization.

## Cost

You pay for every background check, including your first check and each yearly recheck. Each one costs about **\$35**.

## What a report can include

As allowed by law, a report can include:

- Criminal history
- Sex offender registry status
- Identity verification
- Other background information the law permits

## When checks happen

Hooper Howse runs a check before approving you. After that, you're rechecked **every year** while you're a Trainer.

Hooper Howse can also run a check at other times, as the law allows, when reasonably appropriate. Your authorization covers all of these checks.

## If a report affects your eligibility

Any decision not to approve you, or to remove you, based on a report follows the law, including any notice you're owed before and after the decision.
