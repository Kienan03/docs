Hooper Howse is a 13+ platform, so some of your Hoopers will be 13 to 17. These rules apply to every Hooper under 18, and any time you know or reasonably should know a Hooper is a minor.

<Warning>
  Any suspected inappropriate conduct involving a minor means immediate suspension while Hooper Howse investigates. If a violation is confirmed, you're removed from the platform, and Hooper Howse may report it to law enforcement or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.
</Warning>

## Keep communication on Hooper Howse

All contact with a minor Hooper happens through Hooper Howse messaging and features. You can't:

- Contact a minor by phone, text, personal email, social media, DMs, video call, or any other outside channel.
- Ask a minor for a phone number or social handle, or share yours so they can reach you off the platform.
- Meet in person with a minor you met through Hooper Howse.

You **can** contact a minor's parent or guardian through any channel, and you can include a parent or guardian in your conversations with the minor.

## Keep it professional

Every message must relate to basketball training, coaching, development, scheduling, or another legitimate Hooper Howse purpose. Never:

- Send anything romantic, sexual, suggestive, flirtatious, harassing, threatening, discriminatory, or abusive.
- Comment on a minor's body or appearance unless it's about basketball technique.
- Ask a minor to keep anything secret from a parent, guardian, Hooper Howse, or another responsible adult.
- Offer gifts, money, or special favors outside the Hooper Howse service.

## Personal information and media

- Only ask for what you need to coach. Don't ask for a home address, school schedule, or location.
- Never request, send, or keep sexual, intimate, suggestive, or otherwise inappropriate photos or videos involving a minor. Keep training videos to what coaching requires.
- If you receive content that appears to be sexual content involving a minor, don't download, save, forward, or share it. Report it to Hooper Howse immediately.

## Report concerns

Report any suspected inappropriate conduct involving a minor right away, including conduct by another Trainer or user. If a minor is in immediate danger, call 911 or local law enforcement first, then tell Hooper Howse. See [Report a concern](/trainers/reporting-concerns).
