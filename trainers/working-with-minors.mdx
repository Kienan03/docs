Hooper Howse is a 13+ platform, so some of your Hoopers will be 13 to 17. These rules apply to every Hooper under 18 you connect with through Hooper Howse, and any time you know or reasonably should know a Hooper is a minor, but do not apply to Hoopers you have a pre-existing relationship with prior to training on Hooper Howse.

<Warning>
  Any suspected inappropriate conduct involving a minor means immediate suspension while Hooper Howse investigates. If a violation is confirmed, you're removed from the platform, and Hooper Howse may report it to law enforcement or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.
</Warning>

## Keep communication on Hooper Howse

These go further than the contact rules in the [code of conduct](/trainers/code-of-conduct), which apply to every Hooper.

- Communicate with the Hooper through Hooper Howse messaging and features only.
- Don't contact them by phone, text, personal email, social media, outside DMs, or video call unless a parent or guardian is included in the conversation.
- Don't meet in person with a minor you met through Hooper Howse.

You **can** contact a minor's parent or guardian through any channel, and you can include a parent or guardian in your conversations with the minor.

## Boundaries

Everything in the [code of conduct](/trainers/code-of-conduct) applies to minors. On top of that, never ask a minor to keep anything secret from a parent, guardian, Hooper Howse, or another responsible adult.

## Sexual content involving a minor

If you receive content that appears to be sexual content involving a minor, don't download, save, forward, or share it. Report it to Hooper Howse immediately.

## Report concerns

Report any suspected inappropriate conduct involving a minor right away, including conduct by another Trainer or user. If a minor is in immediate danger, call 911 or local law enforcement first, then tell Hooper Howse. See [Report a concern](/trainers/reporting-concerns).
